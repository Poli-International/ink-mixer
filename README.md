## 🎯 Overview

The Tattoo Ink Mixer is a digital lab tool for artists who want to create consistent, custom pigment blends. It allows you to simulate color mixing in real-time, helping you achieve the perfect shade while tracking ratios to ensure reproducibility across multi-session projects.

**Key Use Cases:**

- Creating custom gray wash sets
- Simulating color theory experiments
- Documenting mixing ratios for large-scale pieces

**Target Audience:**

- Tattoo Artists specializing in realism and color work
- Pigment chemists and distributors

---

## ✨ Features

- ✅ **Real-Time Simulation** - See color changes instantly as you adjust ratios.
- ✅ **Ratio Tracking** - Precisely log drops or volume measurements.
- ✅ **Custom Palettes** - Save and retrieve your favorite blends.
- ✅ **Responsive Design** - Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- ✅ **Free & Open Source** - MIT licensed.
